=== SECTION: Programme Overview ===
Programme Name: Master of Science in Digital Financial Technology (MSc DFinTech)
Institution: NUS School of Computing
Official Website: https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/programmes/pg/mdft/

The MSc DFinTech is designed to help students build a strong foundation in computing and finance for careers in the FinTech industry. The programme features elective courses organised along three tracks: Computing Technologies, Financial Data Analytics and Intelligence, and Digital Financial Transactions and Risk Management.

Target Career Outcomes:
- AI software developer
- Data scientist
- FinTech security specialist
- Financial quantitative analyst
- Other similar professions in financial institutions or FinTech firms


=== SECTION: Programme Structure ===
Total Units Required: 52 Units, comprising:
- Core / Essential Courses: 28 Units (7 courses)
- Elective Courses: 12 Units (3 courses)
- Capstone Project (FT5007): 12 Units

Duration:
- Full-time Normal Duration: 1.5 years
- Part-time Normal Duration: 2.5 years
- Maximum Duration (all students): 3 years

Graduation Requirement: Students must fulfil all programme requirements and achieve a minimum final GPA of 3.0.

Workload Per Semester:
- Full-time students: minimum 12 Units, maximum 20 Units per semester. Students who need additional credits may apply to the School of Computing (SOC).
- Part-time students: minimum 4 Units, maximum 12 Units per semester. Students who need additional credits may apply to the SOC.


=== SECTION: Admissions Requirements ===
Academic Qualification:
Applicants must hold one of the following:
- A bachelor's degree in computing; or
- A bachelor's degree in a related discipline such as STEM, Finance, Economics, Statistics, or Business.

Work Experience:
Work experience in FinTech, AI, or Data Analytics is advantageous but not required.

Programming Requirement:
Applicants from non-computing backgrounds should provide evidence of Python knowledge and/or advanced quantitative preparation.

Standardised Test Guidelines (not mandatory; no fixed cutoff):
- GRE: 320 combined verbal and quantitative, with at least 3.5 in analytical writing (guideline only)
- GMAT: 650 and above (guideline only)
- Note: GMAT/GRE is not a mandatory requirement and there is no fixed cutoff score.

English Language Proficiency (required if prior education was not in English):
- TOEFL iBT: minimum 90
- IELTS: minimum 6.0
- Validity: GRE/GMAT valid for 5 years; TOEFL/IELTS valid for 2 years

FT5013 Digital Asset Innovations in Finance (4 units)

FT5014 AI Risk in the Financial Sector (4 units)
Availability: Semester 1 — Not offered | Semester 2 — Not offered
